# Omnichannel Logistics Mobile App

A cross-platform iOS and Android mobile app designed for last-mile delivery drivers operating in high-volume, low-connectivity zones.

## Core Capabilities

- **Offline-First Synchronization**: Local SQLite database with bidirectional change tracking and conflict resolution.
- **Hardware Integrations**: Camera barcode scanning and Bluetooth receipt printing.
- **Live Location Updates**: Background geolocation tracking with battery-efficient geofencing triggers.
